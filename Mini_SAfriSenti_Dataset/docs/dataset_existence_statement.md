# SAfriSenti Dataset Existence Statement

I confirm that the SAfriSenti dataset has been used across several peer-reviewed research outputs forming part of my PhD by publication.

The dataset was developed for multilingual sentiment analysis in South African under-resourced languages. The initial SAfriSenti subset covered English, Sepedi, and Setswana and was introduced in the SIGUL 2022 paper. It was later extended to include Sesotho, isiXhosa, and isiZulu. The dataset contains sentiment labels for positive, negative, and neutral classes and includes both monolingual and code-switched social media text.

The dataset was created through a combination of Twitter data collection, preprocessing, semi-automatic labelling, sentiment lexicons, emoji- and word-based distant supervision, SentiApp-assisted annotation, and native-speaker verification. The dataset and its extensions have been documented in peer-reviewed outputs, including SIGUL 2022, RAIL 2023, BDCC 2024, and PLOS ONE 2025.

Due to Twitter data-sharing restrictions and research ethics considerations, the dataset may not always be distributed publicly in full raw-text form. However, its existence can be verified through dataset file inventory, sample extracts, annotation records, data dictionary, publication evidence, repository/access statement, and experimental outputs.
